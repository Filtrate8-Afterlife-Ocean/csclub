Two Pointers

Link

As an elf working for Santa's factory, you have been instructed to give two presents for each kid. In front of you lie a series of eight boxes, each with a goodness value and sorted in non-descending order. Each kid has a goodness value in which you must find the two boxes that add up to their goodness value. You cannot tell the goodness value of a given box until you have opened it. Please find the two boxes to give to each kid!

Basically it's a find target sum given sorted array problem.

Sliding Window

Link

In front of you lies a series of eight fruits. You would like to consume the maximum number of fruits possible, but you are also a picky eater and can only eat one fruit of each type. Fruits are presented to you one-by-one and you cannot start eating once you start. What is the maximum number of fruits you can select?

Basically it's a find longest subarray with no duplicates problem.
